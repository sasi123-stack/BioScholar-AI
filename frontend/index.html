🔍 Search Features

                    

                        
Semantic Search

                        
Use the search bar to find biomedical articles. Our AI understands context and synonyms, so
                            searching for "heart attack" will also find articles about "myocardial infarction".

                    

                    

                        
Advanced Search

                        
Click the sliders icon in the search bar to access advanced filters including date ranges,
                            article types, and search modes.

                    

                    

                        
Search Modes

                        
Adjust the slider between Keyword (exact matches) and
                            Semantic (AI-powered understanding) to control search behavior.
                        

                    

                




                

                    
📚 Managing Articles

                    

                        
Reading List

                        
Click the bookmark icon on any article to save it to your reading list. Access your saved
                            articles by clicking the bookmark icon in the header.

                    

                    

                        
Citations

                        
Click "Cite" on any article to generate citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, or BibTeX format.
                            Copy to clipboard with one click.

                    

                    

                        
Export

                        
Export your search results or reading list as CSV or BibTeX files for use in reference
                            managers.
